Who we are

CrushLab 3D (“the game”) is a mobile game for Android made by YOUR_NAME (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, you can reach us at sujalamansuri@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

To save your progress, the game keeps the following on your device only:

  • coins, rank, level stars, missions, trophies and crate progress
  • the objects you build and the names you give them
  • your settings, such as sound, vibration, calm mode and avatar

We do not have servers, so this information is never sent to us and we can't see it.

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this saved game data in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can come back if you reinstall. Google manages these backups under its own privacy policy; we can't access them.

To delete your saved game data, use Settings › Reset all progress in the game, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland). Ads appear as videos you choose to watch for in-game rewards, and occasionally as full-screen ads between levels.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may collect and process:

InformationWhat it's used for
Advertising ID and other device identifiersShowing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ads and, where you allow it, personalizing ads
IP address, used to estimate your general location (such as country)Showing ads suitable for your region and preventing fraud
Device and app information (phone model, Android version, app version, language)Displaying ads correctly and measuring ad performance
How you interact with ads (views, taps)Measuring ads, rewarding you for videos and preventing fraud
Crash and diagnostic data from the ads SDKKeeping ads working reliably

This information is collected and handled by Google, not by us. We only receive anonymous totals in our AdMob reports (for example, how many ads were shown). Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information. Under some US state laws, sharing device identifiers for personalized ads may count as “selling” or “sharing” data; you can opt out as explained in the next section.

Your ad choices

In the European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland

When you first open the game, Google's consent message asks whether ads may be personalized and whether data may be stored on your device for ads. You can change your answer any time in the game under Settings › Privacy & ad choices. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Everywhere

  • On your phone, open Settings › Google › Ads (on some phones Settings › Privacy › Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. Without it, ads can't be personalized to you.
  • Manage how Google personalizes ads across services at My Ad Center.
  • Watching reward videos is always optional. You can play the whole game without them.

App permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads. The game itself works offline.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback when things get crushed. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

CrushLab 3D is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in some US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent.

Because we don't collect or store personal data ourselves, we have nothing about you to access or delete. Your saved game stays on your device, and you can delete it as described above. For data processed by Google for ads, use the choices in Your ad choices or Google's tools linked there. You can also contact us and we'll help as far as we can. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security and retention

Your saved game data stays in the app's private storage on your phone until you reset it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses information (for example, if we add new features or ad partners), we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes on Google Play.
